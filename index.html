How it works

Four gentle steps, at your pace.

You are in control at every stage. You may stop at any point, and you never have to use a prescription you receive.

Step 01

Reach out

A free, confidential phone call. We listen, answer questions, and explain every end-of-life option — including palliative care and hospice.

Step 02

Confirm eligibility

Two qualified New Mexico clinicians confirm your prognosis, your capacity to decide, and your ability to self-administer.

Step 03

Plan & prepare

We coordinate the prescription, the pharmacy, and the timing — and help you and your family prepare in the way that feels right.

Step 04

Peace, with support

When and if you choose, at home, on your terms — with your family held and supported throughout, and after.

Cost & access

Cost should never be the reason.

In New Mexico, Medicaid covers the aid-in-dying medication itself, and the underlying medical visits are often covered too. Our fee is for guidance, coordination, and presence — and we offer a sliding scale and a hardship fund so that qualified New Mexicans are never turned away for lack of means.
