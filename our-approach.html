Our approach

The light a luminaria gives is small, steady, and meant to guide a path.

That is how we try to serve: quietly, without pressure, with respect for the person at the center of every decision.

Compassion, not pressure

We present every option, including palliative care and hospice. Many people we speak with never use a prescription — and that is a good outcome. Peace of mind is the point.

Clinical independence

The medical decision always rests with your clinicians. Two qualified New Mexico clinicians confirm eligibility independently — a safeguard the law requires and we take seriously.

The whole family

Dying touches everyone who loves you. We support families through the decision, the day itself if you wish, and the grief that follows.

Why we exist

New Mexico's Elizabeth Whitefield End-of-Life Options Act gives terminally ill adults a legal, dignified choice about how their final chapter unfolds. But knowing the law exists and actually walking the path are two very different things. Willing clinicians can be hard to find. The paperwork, the pharmacy, the timing, the conversations with family — all of it lands on people at the hardest moment of their lives.

Luminaria exists to carry that weight. We are a care-coordination service: we connect qualified New Mexico clinicians with the patients who need them, handle the practical steps, and stay present for the human ones. We are not a hospice and not a pharmacy — we are the steady hand that helps everything else fit together.

What guides us

  • The person decides. Always voluntary, always reversible until the very last choice.
  • Honesty first. We tell you plainly what we can and cannot do, what it costs, and what the law requires.
  • Access over profit. A sliding scale and hardship fund keep the door open regardless of means.
  • Sober, private, unhurried. No marketing that pressures, no rushing a sacred decision.
