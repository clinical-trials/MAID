Why a path of light

On the darkest nights of the year, New Mexicans line their walkways with luminarias — small paper lanterns that guide people gently home. It is a tradition of light offered to others at a threshold.

Rather than the calm-stock-photography every end-of-life site reaches for, this opens on a living high-desert dawn: a path of luminarias flickering softly, stars fading as first light rises over the mesas. It moves slowly, breathes, and settles the viewer. The light — not scenery — is the message.

The scene respects reduced-motion settings (holding a single still frame), needs no photographs or outside assets, and would become the site's signature — carried in smaller touches across every page.
